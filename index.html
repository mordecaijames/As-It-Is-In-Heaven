About the Conference

Prayer Conference is an annual program hosted by Templebreed International, bringing believers together for a time of prayer, worship, and fellowship.

The 2026 edition is themed “AS IT IS IN HEAVEN” and is scheduled to hold from Fri, 11th – Sun, 13th December 2026.

It is an opportunity to seek God together, deepen our fellowship with Him, and align our hearts with His will..
